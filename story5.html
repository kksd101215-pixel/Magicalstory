The Rainbow Bridge

At the edge of the sky, a bridge of radiant rainbows appeared after every storm. Only those with the purest hearts could see it. A little boy named Finn, with a heart full of kindness, stepped onto it and discovered a world where clouds were candy and stars sang lullabies. He met creatures made of light and laughter, who taught him that every act of goodness creates a rainbow in someone’s heart. Finn returned home with joy in his heart, knowing that magic exists wherever love shines.
