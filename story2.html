The Floating Castle

In a kingdom above the clouds, there floated a castle built entirely of rainbow-colored crystals. Princess Mira, the guardian of the skies, used a magical compass to guide the clouds, making sure travelers never got lost. One day, a young boy named Arlo found a glowing feather that led him to the castle. The gates opened, and he was welcomed by sparkling fountains and talking birds. Together, Mira and Arlo discovered a hidden chamber where dreams became reality, and for one magical night, the entire kingdom danced among the stars.
