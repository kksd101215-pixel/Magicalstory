The Moonlit Garden

In the heart of the city, a secret garden blossomed only under the moonlight. Flowers glowed softly and whispered secrets of the stars. A curious girl named Elara stumbled upon it while chasing a glowing butterfly. She discovered that the garden could make wishes come true if she danced under the moonlight. That night, she twirled among sparkling petals, laughing as the moonlight painted her dreams across the sky. From then on, she returned every full moon, filling the world with magic and laughter.
